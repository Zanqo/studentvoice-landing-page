Welcome to StudentVoice

A centralized school complaint management system built by the NEXA Development Team.

Learn more

The problem we identified

Students face significant difficulties when submitting complaints. There is no clear way to follow up, track progress, or receive timely feedback. This leads to delays, repeated physical follow-ups, and unresolved issues.

Our proposed solution: StudentVoice

StudentVoice is a single, centralized platform where students submit complaints, track them with a unique reference number, and receive updates. Administrators receive, assign, monitor, and resolve complaints efficiently.

Key features and benefits

  • Online submission: Submit a complaint easily from any device.
  • Real-time tracking: Follow the status from Submitted, to Under Review, to Assigned, to Resolved.
  • Admin dashboard: Manage and monitor all complaints in one place.
  • Automated updates: Get instant feedback on your complaint's progress.
